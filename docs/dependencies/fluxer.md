# Fluxer

Fluxer is where the team syncs and talks. Public channels are open to the whole community, for questions and non-technical reports. Private channels hold the team's work.

## Join

Create a [Fluxer account](https://fluxer.com/register). Then accept the [community invite](https://fluxer.gg/zrf7UIAg), in the browser or the [desktop app](https://fluxer.com/download).

## Get enrolled

Introduce yourself in the community channels, get to know the team, and share what you want to work on. A project member then enrolls you. You get the `developers` or `ops` role, which opens the private working channels, and access to the GitHub repositories you work on.

### Link your accounts

Enrollment links your GitHub and Fluxer accounts, so CI can mention you on Fluxer. Send a project member these two values in a direct message:

- **Your GitHub username**, shown in your profile's address: `github.com/<username>`.
- **Your Fluxer user ID**, a long number rather than your display name. Click your avatar at the bottom left and choose **Copy user ID**.

The link between your two accounts is private. It is stored encrypted in the team's secrets manager, read only by the CI account that mentions you, and masked in every log. Your main accounts are safe to use. If you would rather keep A-Novel apart from them, you can create dedicated GitHub and Fluxer accounts instead.
