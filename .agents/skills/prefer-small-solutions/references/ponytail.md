# Ponytail attribution

The decision ladder, explicit caller inspection, and recording a shortcut's limit and revisit
condition were informed by [Ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail) at revision
[e3ba2aa6f1e6f0bc4d69eb09c9f0d0a93af56156](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail/tree/e3ba2aa6f1e6f0bc4d69eb09c9f0d0a93af56156).
